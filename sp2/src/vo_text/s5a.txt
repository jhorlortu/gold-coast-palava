Then Amo, The Discerning, who had Aba reading the whole chat to him, asked the question this Special exists for. One witness is kwah-MEH-nah AH-hwoy, who worked beside Rawlings from the P-N-D-C into the N-D-C years and published Working with Rawlings in 2020. He calls him a born leader with a mesmerising charisma, but writes that Rawlings had no plan and no strategy on how to materially develop Ghana, and that in meetings he sometimes looked out of the window or played with his toy planes while technical matters were debated. Rawlings' office replied that the book was generously economical with the truth, and rejected many of its claims, so take it as one insider's account, not the final word.
