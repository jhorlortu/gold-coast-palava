Back at the Nest, the wallet on the shared table belongs to Kwame, The Combative, who arrived this morning with a bet: his uncle had told him the cedi is printed in Switzerland, so he emptied his wallet and held every note up to the window, looking for the words "Made in Switzerland", and found a watermark, a security thread and no country at all. When Baaba, The Ascendant, slid the Crane Currency story across to him, he clung to the ink, and I let him keep half a point; then I told him the Bank of Ghana begs us not to crumple, staple or spray our notes at parties, and The Combative, who had planned a money bouquet for a friend's birthday, went quiet, smoothed every note flat with the side of his hand and put them back in his wallet one by one, the oldest last. I will tell you more about Kwame in good time.
