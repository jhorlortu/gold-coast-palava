Page one, and as the house rule goes, every headline is a question. Our lead: how did a little sea shell, carried here from islands in the Indian Ocean, give its name to the money in your pocket? Is the cedi really printed in Switzerland, as your uncle swears at every funeral? How did Ghana create billions of cedis in 2022 without switching on a single printing press? Why, from December, will Kwame Nkrumah be the only one of the Big Six left on our money? And tucked in the corner like a classified ad: which empire's money did Ghana throw away for decimals almost six years before that empire did the same thing at home?
