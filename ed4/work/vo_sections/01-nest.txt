This afternoon somebody at the Nest has emptied a whole wallet onto the shared table and laid every note in a straight line, the oldest and most crumpled at one end and the crispest at the other, with a single cowrie shell at the head of the row like a chairman, and beside it a yellow sticky note carrying one word, in capital letters: Switzerland. Whose wallet it is, I will tell you before we close. Chale, how the thing dey go? I am Kofi, the Editor, this is the Gold Coast Palava, and this fourth edition costs about fifty megabytes, which, since the thing be say we all no dey hold, is the one price on this paper the cedi cannot change. If you have not watched our first two editions, go back for them first, because the gold Asante owed Britain in the first, and the gold scheme I promised to put a price on in the second, both come back today to collect their bill.
