Then, in nineteen sixty-five, there was one face on every note; now, on Thursday the twelfth of November twenty twenty-six, at Bank Square in Accra, President Mahama launched the Heritage Series: eight notes, eight faces. Agnes Tagwe Kwakupome, remembered as the first Makola trader to open a bank account, on the one cedi; Cardinal Peter Poreku Deri on the two; Kwejir Aggrey on the five; Justice Annie Jagge on the ten; the composer Ephraim Amu, the second of the two men our Amo, The Discerning, is named after, on the twenty; Theodosia Okoh, who designed our flag, on the fifty; Kofi Annan on the hundred; and on the two hundred, the face the soldiers removed in nineteen sixty-seven, now the only one of the Big Six left on our money, Kwame Nkrumah. The notes stay on cotton, not plastic, because of our heat, they are smaller, they carry features to help people with sight loss tell them apart, and from the first of December they circulate beside the old notes, which stay legal tender. The palava came within a day. Theodosia Okoh's nephew said the face on the fifty looked more like her sister, Doctor Letitia Obeng; the Bank replied on the thirteenth that the portrait was built from photographs of Theodosia Okoh herself; and the Minority in Parliament wants to know what the whole exercise cost. And the money in your own pocket? It has never been easier to move money, and it is no easier to keep its value: a three-month treasury bill now pays about four and a half percent a year while prices rise by about five and a half, so money that sits still is quietly shrinking. And here is the item in the corner of page one. Ghana dropped pounds, shillings and pence for the decimal cedi in July nineteen sixty-five, and Britain itself did not go decimal until the fifteenth of February nineteen seventy-one, so the colony counted in tens almost six years before the empire did.
