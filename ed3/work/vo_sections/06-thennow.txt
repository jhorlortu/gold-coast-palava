On the sixteenth of November 2019, in Ho, the Homeland Study Group Foundation, led by Charles Kormi Koo-jor-jee, then in his eighties, declared a state of Western Togoland, arguing that the 1956 union was never properly formed; and at dawn on the twenty-fifth of September 2020, armed men linked to the movement raided the police stations at Meh-peh and Ah-veh-yee-meh and blocked the roads into the Volta. The state answers that a United Nations plebiscite settled the matter, the Volta chiefs disowned the declaration, and the courts have jailed members of the armed group; yet in May 2025 the ECOWAS Court ordered Ghana to pay seventy-five thousand dollars to thirty members held for long periods without trial.
